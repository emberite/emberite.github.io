Beast FeastDaggerheart Core Rulebook

Hotpot

Cooking as a shared, animated scene at the table.

Throw ingredients into the pot, roll the flavor dice, collect matches and serve the Meal Rating to the party. Every connected player watches the cook live, and every dish lands in the party cookbook.

v0.5.5 Released 24 Nov 2026 MIT

  • The kitchen

    Prep, cook and serve in one window: the pantry, the pot, the flavor dice and the recipe card, with Last Drop, Risky, Spicy and Built for Speed handled for you.

  • Cooking together

    Call everyone to the pot and the kitchen opens for every player, mirroring each roll, match and discard.

  • Ingredient Generator

    Harvest a beast by its HP and tier, roll a bloom on the Hope Die, or build a custom ingredient, then hand out servings in one click.

  • 45 food-themed adversaries

    From Fryling Swarms to the Mac and Cheese Colossus, across Tiers 1–4, each listing what it yields.

Five Banners BurningDaggerheart Core Rulebook

Five Banners

Factions, relationships and reputation for the five nations of Althas.

The GM builds the factions of the campaign and hands each one to the characters as they meet it. Every character sheet gets a Factions tab showing who they know, how those factions feel about each other and where the character stands with each.

v0.3.0 Released 24 Nov 2026 MIT

  • Factions tab

    A new tab on every character sheet with banners, relationships and a reputation track per faction.

  • The Five Nations in one click

    Armada, Hilltop, Jesthaen, Polaris and Voldaen, with their banners and the relationship ratings from the campaign frame.

  • Objective countdowns

    Major and minor faction objectives run on the system’s own countdowns, hidden from players until you reveal them.

  • Reputation (homebrew)

    Marks for deeds done for or against a faction shift a character from Nemeses to Close Allies.

Journey to HorizonDaggerheart: Hope & Fear

Horizon

A hex crawl of New Valloren, rolled on the fly from the campaign’s dice tables.

Horizon builds the continent, rolls the wilderness as the party approaches, and runs the journey hex by hex: travel days, Encounter Dice, ocean weather, rests, Soul Echoes and the Doom Track. The GM sees the key map; the players see what they’ve crossed.

v0.1.1 Released 24 Nov 2026 MIT

  • Map builder

    A continent from Marrogate to the Cliffs of Horizon, with sanctuaries and nine Soul Echoes placed as the book suggests.

  • Wilderness on the fly

    Habitat, region size, encounter, terrain and rumor are rolled for each region as the party draws near.

  • The journey

    Move the token: travel days, Encounter Dice and ocean weather are rolled for every hex entered, with GM-only cards.

  • Rests, Echoes and Doom

    Short-rest limits, the Locate Soul Echoes downtime move, levelling with the Vessel of Light, and the Doom Track.

Reign of the WeredragonDaggerheart: Hope & Fear

Weredragon

Build the weredragon queen together, then read her fate in the stars.

Two shared table apps for session zero, the Villain Builder and the Astromancer’s Night Sky Reading, plus compendiums for Forlorne Lykona, her weredrakes, the two curses and the curse-breaking Keys to Victory.

v0.2.0 Released 24 Nov 2026 MIT

  • Villain Builder

    The whole table draws folded slips or rolls the prompts, answers them in turn, and the answers are written to a journal.

  • Night Sky Reading

    The players roll the stars, the GM reads the sky into a constellation, and the five revelations appear for everyone.

  • Forlorne Lykona

    Her Faun and Dragon forms, with Transform swapping the token while keeping HP, Stress and conditions.

  • Curses, automated

    Weredrake and Petrified curses as status conditions that reshape or petrify the token, broken by the Keys to Victory.

Installing a module

  1. 1

    Get the requirements

    Foundry VTT v14, the Daggerheart system 2.12 or later, and the official content module the frame comes from (Core Rulebook or Hope & Fear). The modules load that content at runtime and include none of it.

  2. 2

    Paste the manifest

    In Foundry’s setup screen, open Add-on Modules → Install Module, paste the module’s manifest URL from above and click Install.

  3. 3

    Enable it in your world

    Turn it on under Manage Modules. Its tools appear in the Daggerheart sidebar menu, under the campaign frame’s name.
